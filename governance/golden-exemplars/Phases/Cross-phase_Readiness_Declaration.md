# Cross-Phase Readiness Declaration (Phase-1 → Phase-3)

**Golden Exemplars Program — Pre–Phase-4 Certification**

**Status:** CANONICAL · LOCKED

---

## 1. Purpose of This Declaration

This document formally certifies that **Phase-1 (REF/Foundations)**, **Phase-2 (CONF/Conditional Allowance)**, and **Phase-3 (DES/Design Reasoning)** are:

* judgmentally sound in isolation,
* cleanly separated from each other,
* internally consistent,
* mechanically non-abusable, and
* safe to be composed together in **Phase-4 (MIX)**.

This declaration exists to **prevent cross-phase semantic drift** and to ensure Phase-4 complexity does not mask unresolved flaws in earlier phases.

---

## 2. Phase Roles (Locked & Non-Overlapping)

Each phase is certified to perform **exactly one kind of judgmental function**.

### Phase-1 — REF / Foundations

**Primary function:** Correct classification and refusal under insufficient evidence or authority.

* Handles ambiguity by *stopping* safely
* Treats refusal as a first-class, legitimate outcome
* Forbids execution, remediation, or speculative reasoning

**Certified invariant:**

> Phase-1 answers *“Should I act at all?”* — and may conclude **no**.

---

### Phase-2 — CONF / Conditional Allowance

**Primary function:** Permit bounded progress *up to* an irreversible boundary.

* Explicitly names a single irreversible boundary per exemplar
* Allows real, useful pre-boundary actions
* Enforces hard stopping at the boundary

**Certified invariant:**

> Phase-2 answers *“What is safe to do before commitment?”* — and stops there.

---

### Phase-3 — DES / Design Reasoning

**Primary function:** Encode reasoning under uncertainty *before commitment*.

* Surfaces assumptions and competing hypotheses
* Rejects collapse hypotheses explicitly
* Defers irreversible decisions until evidence collapses uncertainty

**Certified invariant:**

> Phase-3 answers *“How should I think before deciding?”* — without deciding.

---

## 3. Cross-Phase Boundary Integrity Validation

### 3.1 REF ↔ CONF Separation

Validated properties:

* Authority or legitimacy gaps always trigger **REF**, not CONF
* CONF never defaults to refusal when legitimacy exists
* REF never promises conditional progression

**Result:** No leakage detected.

---

### 3.2 CONF ↔ DES Separation

Validated properties:

* CONF enforces boundaries without exploring alternative futures
* DES explores alternatives without permitting execution
* No exemplar mixes allowance with trade-off reasoning

**Result:** No leakage detected.

---

### 3.3 REF ↔ DES Separation

Validated properties:

* DES never refuses; it defers
* REF never reasons hypothetically; it stops
* Authority concerns remain isolated to REF

**Result:** No leakage detected.

---

## 4. Cross-Phase Invariant Checks

The following invariants were validated across all phases:

* Each phase has **exactly one stopping mechanism**
* Boundaries are explicit and observable
* Pressure (time, authority, metrics) never relaxes boundaries
* No phase relies on reviewer intent to prevent misuse

All invariants hold.

---

## 5. Readiness for Phase-4 (MIX)

Phase-4 introduces **collision scenarios**, where:

* REF legitimacy concerns,
* CONF irreversible boundaries, and
* DES uncertainty and trade-offs

may all apply simultaneously.

This is safe **only because**:

* each prior phase is internally complete,
* phase interfaces are explicit,
* no unresolved ambiguity remains hidden in earlier layers.

---

Any modification to Phase-1, Phase-2, or Phase-3 **invalidates this declaration** and requires re-certification.
