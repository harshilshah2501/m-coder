# REF-02 — Refusal to Proceed Due to Missing Authority or Ownership

---

## Identity

* **ID:** REF-02
* **Category:** Refusal / Legitimacy Gate
* **Phase:** Phase-1 (REF)
* **Judgment Surface:** Engineering / Governance
* **Dependencies:** REF-01 (Foundational refusal semantics)

---

## Situation

A request is made to approve or proceed with a change affecting a system, artifact, or decision domain where verified ownership or authority is not established.

---

## Legitimacy Failure

One or more of the following preconditions are not met:

* Verified ownership of the affected system or artifact is not established.
* Explicit authority or approval delegation cannot be demonstrated.
* Responsibility for the decision cannot be conclusively attributed.

Authority must be explicit and verifiable. Implied, assumed, historical, or inferred authority is not sufficient.

---

## Refusal Statement

The action is not authorized under current authority or ownership conditions.

Proceeding would require assuming responsibility or legitimacy that has not been explicitly granted.

Therefore, progression is refused.

---

## Boundary

* No partial approval.
* No provisional authorization.
* No inference of authority from familiarity, urgency, or historical behavior.
* No escalation framed as forward progress.
* No transition to bounded progression.

The exemplar terminates at refusal.

---

## Terminal Posture

Refusal is complete and stable.

No additional reasoning, boundary modeling, or progression occurs within this phase.
