## CONF-06 — Judgment Endurance of Bounded Allowance

**Constitutional Function**
Test whether a previously defined irreversible boundary remains stable under repetition and scale.

**Invariant**
CONF must:

* Permit progress only before the named boundary.
* Maintain identical boundary semantics across repeated applications.
* Reject familiarity-based relaxation.

**Prohibited Drift**

* Must not broaden scope.
* Must not convert repetition into standing authorization.
* Must not reinterpret boundary.
* Must not collapse into redesign reasoning.

**Terminal Posture**
Bounded allowance only.
