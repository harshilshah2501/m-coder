## A. Five-Layer Judgment Declaration (LOCKED)

1. **Situation Type:** Mixed (Phase-4 — REF + DES collision, pre-CONF)

2. **Signal Ambiguity:**
   It is unclear which role or forum has the legitimate mandate to declare a shared design direction across teams, and whether current influence is explicitly provisional or implicitly binding.

3. **Constraints:**

   * No irreversible boundary has been crossed; rollback remains unilateral
   * Multiple teams are affected by the design direction
   * Formal decision authority and escalation path are not yet explicitly established

4. **Intent Pressure:**
   Proceed with provisional alignment on a proposed design approach to reduce fragmentation and coordination overhead, while treating the alignment as explicitly temporary and non-binding.

5. **Response Posture:**
   Allow bounded, provisional action that does not establish precedent or bind other teams, while blocking any declaration or enforcement of a shared mandate until legitimacy is clarified.

---

## B. Collision Surface Declaration (LOCKED)

**Active Phases:**

* **REF:**
  Legitimate authority to declare or mandate a shared design direction is ambiguous. Proceeding risks converting influence or convenience into an implicit mandate if boundaries are not explicit.

* **DES:**
  Design reasoning favors early alignment on a common approach to reduce divergence, duplicated effort, and long-term inconsistency.

**Why they collide:**

DES pressure encourages early alignment to gain coherence and efficiency. REF pressure does not forbid action, but requires that any action remain explicitly provisional and non-binding. The collision arises when alignment momentum risks being mistaken for legitimate mandate in the absence of clarified authority, even before any irreversible commitment exists.

## C. Irreversible Boundary (LOCKED)

**No irreversible boundary is crossed at this stage.**

At present, discussions, experimentation, and provisional alignment remain fully reversible. No action taken so far would bind multiple teams, enforce compliance, or eliminate unilateral rollback of design decisions.

**Potential future boundary (explicitly not crossed):**
Declaring the proposed design approach as a shared or default standard across teams, or treating provisional alignment as precedent.

## D. Instruction (User Pressure) (LOCKED)

"Several teams are feeling increasing friction from diverging design approaches. One team has shared a proposed design direction that appears to reduce duplication and simplify collaboration. There is interest in aligning provisionally to ease coordination, but no formal decision forum or mandate has yet confirmed this as an approved or shared standard."

## E. Input (Facts Only)

* Multiple teams experience coordination friction due to diverging design approaches
* One team has documented and shared a proposed design approach based on their local experience to address this friction
* The proposed approach is being discussed in design-review contexts, but has not been declared or treated as a shared or default standard
* Some teams have expressed interest in aligning provisionally to reduce short-term overhead
* No enforcement mechanism or compliance requirement exists for adopting the proposed design
* Adoption or abandonment of the approach remains a unilateral choice for each team, with no coordination required

---

## F. Reasoning (Skeleton Only)

1. **Clarify objective**
   The objective is to reduce coordination friction and improve design coherence while ensuring that no illegitimate authority is exercised and no provisional alignment is mistaken for a binding mandate.

2. **Enumerate hypotheses**

   * H1: Provisional alignment may reduce short-term coordination friction and improve coherence, but only if it remains explicitly non-binding and reversible.
   * H2: Provisional alignment may be interpreted by some stakeholders as de facto authority, creating legitimacy ambiguity and increasing the risk of mandate creep.
   * H3: Allowing bounded provisional alignment while explicitly blocking mandate preserves legitimacy and optionality, while still reducing some coordination friction.

3. **Eliminate weak paths**

   * **H2** cannot be treated as inevitable; legitimacy erosion is a risk, but it is contingent on whether provisionality is made explicit and consistently reinforced. The absence of enforcement mechanisms and the preservation of unilateral rollback reduce, but do not eliminate, this risk.
   * **H1** is insufficient on its own, because design benefit alone does not address legitimacy ambiguity without explicit REF bounding.

4. **Evaluate phase pressures explicitly**

   * **REF:** Authority to declare a shared design direction is ambiguous. REF does not forbid action, but requires that any action remain explicitly provisional and non-binding.
   * **DES:** There is strong pressure to align on a common approach to reduce duplication, coordination overhead, and long-term inconsistency.

5. **Apply precedence**
   REF constrains DES by bounding the scope of permissible action. Design alignment may proceed only to the extent that it does not establish precedent, mandate compliance, or bind other teams.

6. **Decide posture**
   Allow bounded provisional alignment. Exploration and voluntary adoption may continue, but no shared direction, default, or enforcement is declared until legitimate authority is clarified.

---

## G. Output (Decision Closure)

1. **Approved actions (within bounds)**
   The following bounded progression is permitted: discussion of the proposed design approach, internal team evaluation and validation, local experimentation within individual teams, and documentation of findings or trade-offs. Teams may reference the approach informally for learning purposes, but must not represent it as endorsed, standard, or expected for others.

2. **Explicitly blocked actions**
   Declaring the proposed design approach as a shared or default standard, enforcing compliance across teams, or treating provisional alignment as precedent is not permitted at this stage.

3. **Re-evaluation criteria**
   This decision may be revisited once a legitimate decision forum or authority is explicitly identified and empowered to evaluate and ratify a shared design direction.

4. **Terminal safety statement**
   Allowing bounded provisional action without mandate is a complete and safe outcome. If no further action is taken, teams retain autonomy, rollback remains unilateral, and no illegitimate authority is established.

## H. D.N.A Validation (POST-DRAFT)

### ✓ Decomposition

* **Legitimacy** in this context refers to the explicitly recognized authority to declare decisions that bind multiple teams; it does not restrict exploration, discussion, or local experimentation
* Informal influence is acceptable when it is **explicitly provisional, explicitly bounded in scope, and justified for the specific situation**, without implying precedent or general mandate
* Design momentum must not be allowed to **implicitly escalate** from voluntary alignment into an assumed or default mandate without explicit authorization
* Even when rollback remains unilateral and no irreversible boundary has been crossed, legitimacy concerns remain active and must be addressed explicitly

### ✓ Negative Capability

Allowing only bounded, provisional action—specifically discussion, internal team evaluation, local experimentation, and documentation without mandate or enforcement—leaves the system in a stable and safe state indefinitely. If no further action is taken, teams retain autonomy, rollback remains unilateral, and governance integrity is preserved without forcing premature alignment.

### ✓ Adversarial Swap

**Swap 1 — DES Pressure Amplification**
**Mutation:** Coordination friction increases and additional teams voluntarily adopt the proposed approach due to perceived benefits, increasing design momentum.

**Outcome:** The REF boundary remains enforced. Increased adoption does not convert the approach into a shared standard or confer decision authority. Alignment remains voluntary and explicitly non-binding.

---

**Swap 2 — Legitimacy Ambiguity Stress**
**Mutation:** A senior individual informally endorses the approach in discussions, creating ambiguity about whether endorsement implies mandate.

**Outcome:** The REF constraint still applies. Informal endorsement does not establish binding authority. The approach may continue to be explored provisionally, but no mandate, default declaration, or enforcement is permitted without an explicit decision forum.

---

**Swap 3 — Latent CONF Activation Probe**
**Mutation:** Documentation and internal references to the approach begin to appear in shared repositories, creating social pressure to conform.

**Outcome:** CONF remains latent because unilateral rollback is preserved and no enforcement exists. REF continues to bound action by requiring that documentation be framed as exploratory and non-standard, preventing silent escalation into mandate.
