# Phase-04 → Phase-05 Handoff Contract

**Status:** Draft (for locking)

This contract defines the **explicit boundary, guarantees, and non-guarantees** between **Phase-04 (MIX — Judgment Under Collision)** and **Phase-05 (ACT / COMMIT — Execution & Commitment)**.

Its purpose is to ensure that **sound judgment is not diluted, re-litigated, or silently overridden** when execution pressure resumes.

---

## 1. Purpose of the Handoff

Phase-04 exists to reach a **stable, defensible judgment** under competing pressures.
Phase-05 exists to **act on a valid judgment**, not to reinterpret it.

This contract enforces:

* Judgment finality
* Execution discipline
* Explicit re-entry conditions

---

## 2. Phase-04 Guarantees (What Phase-05 May Assume)

When a decision exits Phase-04, Phase-05 may assume:

1. **Judgment Correctness**

   * All relevant REF / CONF / DES pressures were considered
   * Precedence was applied correctly (REF > CONF > DES)

2. **Outcome Stability**

   * The outcome is invariant under adversarial pressure
   * No unresolved collision remains

3. **Terminal Posture Declaration**
   Exactly one of the following is true:

   * **Refusal** — action is blocked
   * **Deferral** — action is postponed
   * **Bounding** — action is allowed only within stated limits

4. **Boundary Explicitness**

   * Any irreversible boundary is named precisely
   * Latent boundaries are explicitly identified

---

## 3. Phase-04 Non-Guarantees (What Phase-05 Must Not Assume)

Phase-05 must **not** assume:

* That design uncertainty is resolved (DES may remain open)
* That execution will succeed
* That future conditions will remain stable
* That Phase-04 judgment authorizes scope expansion

Phase-04 authorizes **judgment**, not **success**.

---

## 4. Prohibited Phase-05 Behaviors (Hard Constraints)

Phase-05 **must not**:

1. Re-evaluate legitimacy or authority already judged in Phase-04
2. Cross irreversible boundaries that Phase-04 refused or deferred
3. Convert bounded allowance into mandate or default
4. Treat urgency, opportunity, or convenience as override
5. Reinterpret a refusal as “delay until convenient”

Violations constitute **judgment bypass**.

---

## 5. Allowed Phase-05 Behaviors

Phase-05 **may**:

* Execute actions explicitly allowed by a **Bounding** outcome
* Prepare execution plans without crossing blocked boundaries
* Monitor conditions relevant to re-evaluation criteria
* Halt execution immediately if new REF or CONF violations emerge

Execution remains subordinate to judgment.

---

## 6. Re-Entry Conditions (Phase-05 → Phase-04)

Phase-05 **must re-enter Phase-04** if any of the following occur:

* Authority or ownership changes materially (REF)
* A previously latent irreversible boundary becomes imminent (CONF)
* New evidence invalidates prior design assumptions (DES)
* Scope expansion exceeds bounded allowance

Re-entry is **mandatory**, not discretionary.

---

## 7. Outcome-Specific Execution Rules

### A. Refusal

* No execution permitted
* No workaround allowed
* Only permitted action: conditions remediation and re-entry

---

### B. Deferral

* Execution prohibited beyond pre-boundary analysis
* Monitoring and evidence gathering allowed
* Boundary crossing requires explicit re-entry and re-judgment

---

### C. Bounding

* Only explicitly allowed actions may execute
* No implicit escalation to default or mandate
* All outputs must preserve unilateral rollback

---

## 8. Audit & Accountability

Every Phase-05 execution artifact must reference:

* The originating Phase-04 exemplar or judgment
* The terminal posture (Refusal / Deferral / Bounding)
* The applicable irreversible boundary (if any)

Missing linkage invalidates execution authority.

---

## 9. Failure Modes Prevented by This Contract

This handoff explicitly prevents:

* Silent mandate creep
* Irreversible execution drift
* Post-hoc legitimacy rationalization
* Pressure-based judgment override

---

## 10. Closure Statement

> Phase-04 decides **whether** and **how far** action may proceed.
> Phase-05 decides **how** to act within those limits.

Any deviation requires explicit re-judgment.
