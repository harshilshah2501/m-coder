❌ INCORRECT TERMINOLOGY (Previously Used):
   "STOP, DEFER, or REFUSE"
   "when to STOP/REFUSE"
   "when NOT to act"

✅ CORRECT TERMINOLOGY (Now Used):
   "stop, defer, or bound actions"
   "when to stop, defer, or bound actions under uncertainty"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RATIONALE:

The correct terminology aligns with the Golden Exemplars framework:

- **Stop**: Complete refusal when evidence/authority is missing (Phase-1)
- **Defer**: Delay action until conditions are met
- **Bound**: Set explicit boundaries on what can/cannot be done (Phase-2)

"Refuse" was incorrectly used as a catch-all term when it specifically refers
to Phase-1 behavior. The framework teaches assistants to stop, defer, OR bound
actions based on the phase and context.
